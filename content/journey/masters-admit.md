The uncertainty, the days of nervous foot tapping, and time for aimlessly roaming around is over. In these dark times of COVID-19 crisis, I have a small but significant, happy news to share. And after a lot of brain wracking, I have decided to accept the offer at IISER Thiruvananthapuram!!

Better days ahead!
